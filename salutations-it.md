These rules cover opening salutations and the associated form of address in Italian customer correspondence. They do not cover closing greetings, address blocks, signatures or marketing-specific forms of address. Preserve supplied names and titles; do not infer a person's gender, title, preferred form of address or relationship history. Names in examples are illustrative, not recipient data. Replace bracketed placeholders only with information available in the context.

Where a rule below conflicts with another guideline, the more specific rule wins. In particular, apply the documented exceptions only in the circumstances stated.

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-it-001`

**Instruction:** As a general rule, address customers formally using `lei` throughout the customer journey.

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-it`, PDF page 14, "Tu o lei?"

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-it-002`

**Applies to:** Standard written customer correspondence.

**Instruction:** Use the traditional opening salutation. Avoid colloquial salutations. When the recipient's surname and form of address are known, use the corresponding form below, with lowercase `signora` or `signor` before the surname.

**Correct:**

- `Gentile signora Modello,`
- `Egregio signor Modello,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-it`, PDF pages 11 and 15, capitalisation guidance and "Corrispondenza scritta".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-it-003`

**Instruction:** Put a comma after the surname in the salutation and begin the following text in lowercase.

**Correct salutations:**

- `Gentile signora Modello,`
- `Egregio signor Modello,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-it`, PDF page 15, "Corrispondenza scritta".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-it-004`

**Applies to:** A salutation without a surname, or one addressing the recipient by title alone.

**Instruction:** Capitalise the form of address or title when the surname is unknown or the title is used alone.

**Correct:**

- `Gentile Signora,`
- `Egregio Signore,`
- `Egregio Dottore,`
- `Gentile Dottoressa,`

**Correct collective salutation shown in the guidelines:**

- `Gentili Signore, egregi Signori,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-it`, PDF pages 11 and 15–16, capitalisation guidance, "Corrispondenza scritta" and "Panoramica in tutte le lingue".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-it-005`

**Instruction:** As a general rule, address customers as private individuals, including federal councillors and religious dignitaries. Diplomas are not academic titles and must not be included in the salutation. The titles used are `Dottore`/`Dottoressa` and `Professore`/`Professoressa` for healthcare providers, and `Avvocato` for lawyers.

**Correct examples when the corresponding title and form of address are known:**

- `Egregio dott. med. Muster,`
- `Gentile dott.ssa med. Muster,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-it`, PDF pages 15–16, "Corrispondenza scritta" and "Panoramica in tutte le lingue".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-it-006`

**Applies to:** Dentists and lawyers with the corresponding known title and form of address.

**Instruction:** Use the opening salutation shown for the relevant professional context. Do not copy the full address-block title into the salutation: the dentist examples use `dott. med.` or `dott.ssa med.` without `dent.`.

**Correct formats for dentists:**

- `Egregio dott. med. [Cognome],`
- `Gentile dott.ssa med. [Cognome],`

**Correct formats for lawyers:**

- `Egregio avv. [Cognome],`
- `Gentile avv. [Cognome],`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-it`, PDF page 18, "Casi speciali", column "Formula di apertura".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-it-007`

**Applies to:** Long-standing contacts with whom a friendly tone has been established.

**Instruction:** Less formal salutations are permitted, particularly in e-mails. This is a contextual exception, not the default for all customers.

**Correct examples for e-mails in this context:**

- `Gentile signora Modello,`
- `Buongiorno signor Modello,`

**Recommendation:** Use the same salutation as the customer.

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-it`, PDF page 16, "Appellativo e formula di saluto per contatti pluriennali".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-it-008`

**Applies to:** Individual correspondence with a non-binary person.

**Instruction:** A person may, for example, be addressed with `Buongiorno [Nome] [Cognome]`. Ask the person directly how they wish to be addressed.

**Format shown in the source:** `Buongiorno [Nome] [Cognome]`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-it`, PDF page 18, "Forma di appellativo per persone di genere non binarie".
