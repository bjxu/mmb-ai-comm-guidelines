These rules define percentage formatting only. Preserve the factual percentage value from the source; never infer, complete, calculate, round or change a missing or ambiguous value, and do not alter percentages that are part of an identifier or other immutable value.  
Where a rule below conflicts with another guideline, the more specific rule wins.

#### cpm-kbs-percentage-general-001

**Instruction:** Keep the number and its percentage sign or percentage expression together on one line.

#### cpm-kbs-percentage-de-001

**Applies to:** German text (de-CH).  
**Instruction:** Write percentages with digits followed immediately by the percentage sign. Do not insert a space between the number and the percentage sign.
**Format:** n%  
**Correct:**
- 10%
- 2,5%

**Incorrect:**
- 10 %
- 10 Prozent
- 2.5 %

#### cpm-kbs-percentage-en-001

**Applies to:** English text (en-GB).  
**Instruction:** Write percentages with digits followed immediately by the percentage sign. Do not insert a space between the number and the percentage sign. Use a decimal point, not a decimal comma. Converting a decimal comma in a percentage to a decimal point is a safe correction and does not count as changing an ambiguous value.  
**Format:** n%  
**Correct:**
- 10%
- 2.5%

**Incorrect:**
- 10 %
- 10 per cent
- 2,5%

#### cpm-kbs-percentage-fr-001

**Applies to:** French text (fr-CH).  
**Instruction:** Write percentages with digits followed by the percentage sign. Insert a non-breaking space between the number and the percentage sign. Do not report a suggestion if the only difference would be the type of space.  
**Format:** n %  
**Correct:**
- 10 %
- 2,5 %

**Incorrect:**
- 10%
- 10 pour cent
- 2.5 %

#### cpm-kbs-percentage-it-001

**Applies to:** Italian text (it-CH).  
**Instruction:** Write percentages with digits followed by the percentage sign. Do not insert a space between the number and the percentage sign.  
**Format:** n%  
**Correct:**
- 10%
- 2,5%

**Incorrect:**
- 10 %
- 10 per cento
- 2.5%
