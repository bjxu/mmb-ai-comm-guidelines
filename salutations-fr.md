These rules cover opening salutations in French customer correspondence. They do not cover closing greetings, address blocks, signatures or marketing-specific forms of address. Preserve supplied names and titles; do not infer a person's gender, title or preferred form of address. Names in examples are illustrative, not recipient data.

The correspondence channel determines whether a surname is included. Do not apply a letter-specific rule to an e-mail or myHelsana message, or vice versa. If the channel needed to choose the salutation is unknown, request clarification rather than assuming it.

Where a rule below conflicts with another guideline, the more specific rule wins.

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-fr-001`

**Applies to:** Letters.

**Instruction:** Use an opening salutation without the recipient's name, even when the name is known.

**Correct:**

- `Madame,`
- `Monsieur,`
- `Madame, Monsieur,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-fr`, PDF page 17, "Correspondance écrite".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-fr-002`

**Applies to:** E-mails and myHelsana messages.

**Instruction:** Use a personalised opening salutation in the form `Bonjour` followed by the recipient's form of address and surname, with a comma at the end.

**Correct:**

- `Bonjour Monsieur Exemple,`
- `Bonjour Madame Exemple,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-fr`, PDF page 17, "Correspondance écrite".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-fr-003`

**Instruction:** As a general rule, address customers as private individuals, including federal councillors and religious dignitaries. The only titles used are `Docteur`/`Docteure` and `Professeur`/`Professeure` for healthcare providers, and `Maître` for lawyers.

**Correct examples for letters when the corresponding title is known:**

- `Docteur,`
- `Docteure,`
- `Professeur,`
- `Professeure,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-fr`, PDF pages 17–18, "Correspondance écrite" and "Vue d'ensemble pour toutes les langues".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-fr-004`

**Instruction:** Capitalise courtesy forms and honorific titles in the salutation.

**Correct:**

- `Madame,`
- `Monsieur,`
- `Bonjour Madame Exemple,`
- `Bonjour Monsieur Exemple,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-fr`, PDF page 13, capitalisation rule 16, and PDF page 17, "Correspondance écrite".

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-fr-005`

**Applies to:** Individual correspondence by e-mail or myHelsana with a non-binary person who asks for this to be taken into account.

**Instruction:** Use simply `Bonjour,`.

**Correct:**

- `Bonjour,`

**Source:** `CPM-KB-SRC-002-fr`, PDF pages 17 and 20, "Correspondance écrite" and "Formule d'appel pour les personnes non-binaires".
