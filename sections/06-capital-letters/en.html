Capital letters

Use capital (big) letters:

  • At the beginning of a sentence and after a full stop (including question and exclamation marks)
  • In direct speech after the introductory comma, at the beginning of the quotation
  • After an ellipsis (dot-dot-dot) … (if a new sentence follows)
  • Days, months and public holidays (but not seasons): Sunday, March, Christmas, summer
  • Names of people, institutions and places (proper nouns), including stars and planets: Mary, the Foreign Office, North Africa, the United States, The Pole Star, Mars
  • People’s titles: Mr Smith, the Managing Director, the Prime Minister
  • Nationalities, countries and regions, languages, ethnic groups and religions: Michelle’s French but she lives in Northern Ireland. I speak German. He’s Jewish.
  • Acronyms (a word formed from the initial letters of other words): UNESCO, SIM, NATO
