Linguistic equality

You can achieve linguistic equality by using collective nouns. They describe a group of people, places, things or concepts. In British English, collective nouns are sometimes treated as plural, meaning they are conjugated with a plural verb (e.g. “staff are”).

Examples

  • the staff / personnel (instead of: employees, workers)
  • the cleaning crew, the cabin crew, etc.
  • the sales team, the marketing team, etc.
  • the translation service (instead of: translators)
  • the management (instead of: managing director)
  • the police (instead of: police officers)
