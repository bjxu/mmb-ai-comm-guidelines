Amounts in Swiss francs/CHF

CHF is the international currency code for Swiss francs (ISO 4217).

In most contexts, amounts of money are stated using the ISO code, CHF. In certain publications, currencies may be written out in running text for stylistic reasons, and must be written out where no figure is given. However, they should never be written out if the stated amount has a decimal point. Amounts in tables should be written with a decimal point.

  • with cents CHF 2.50
  • without cents CHF 200.– (with dash*) Example: You could win CHF 200.–
  • in tables: CHF 6.00 (not CHF 6)
* How to create a dash (en dash) in Word: press Ctrl and the minus symbol on the numeric keypad (-). In all other programmes: press Alt and type 0150.
Exception for marketing: Format of amounts in Swiss francs in texts for publication

Currencies

Amounts in running text:

  • EUR with cents: EUR 2.50
  • EUR without cents: EUR 200.– (with dash)
  • USD with cents: USD 2.50
  • USD without cents: USD 200.– (with dash)

Amounts in tables:

  • EUR with cents: EUR 6.50
  • EUR without cents: EUR 6.00 (always with two zeroes)
  • USD with cents: USD 6.50
  • USD without cents: USD 6.00 (always with two zeroes)
