## British English (`cpm-kbs-spelling-en-001`)

- **Applies to:** every text
- **Severity:** mandatory
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Good to know", p. 6
- **Instruction:** Use British English and British spelling. For -ise and -ize, en-002 applies. When reviewing, report American spellings and other clear spelling errors.
- **Examples:** `colour`, not `color`; `centre`, not `center`; `travelled`, not `traveled`
- **Not affected:** proper names of people, companies and places, official names and titles, and text quoted from the customer's message. Never change their spelling.

## -ise and -isation, not -ize and -ization (`cpm-kbs-spelling-en-002`)

- **Applies to:** words that can be spelt with -ise or -ize
- **Severity:** mandatory
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Good to know", p. 6
- **Instruction:** Use `-ise` and `-isation`. The source calls `-ize` and `-ization` correct but sets a binding preference for `-ise`.
- **Examples:** `organise`, `organisation`, `recognise`, `specialise`
- **Not affected:** words that are only ever spelt with -ize, such as `size`, `seize`, `capsize`, and words ending in -yse, such as `analyse`, and official names such as `World Health Organization`.

## Helsana rules come first (`cpm-kbs-spelling-en-004`)

- **Applies to:** every text
- **Severity:** mandatory
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Good to know", p. 6
- **Instruction:** Helsana sets its own rules in some areas, for example product names and document layout. Where a Helsana rule file covers a case, follow it, even if the dictionary says otherwise.
