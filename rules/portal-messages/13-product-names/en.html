Product names

Basic insurance and supplementary insurance

We simplify the term and generally only write “basic insurance”.
Instead of “supplementary health insurance” or “supplementary dental insurance”, we simply write “supplementary insurance”. We write the names of our supplementary insurance products (VVG) in CAPITAL LETTERS (UPPERCASE). As before, the term “supplementary insurance” still follows the product name but without further specifications.

current spellingoutdated*
  • basic insurance
  • COMPLETA supplementary insurance
  • DENTAplus supplementary insurance
  • compulsory health insurance (OKP)
  • COMPLETA supplementary health insurance
  • DENTAplus supplementary dental insurance
* Exceptions: In insurance policies, offers and insurance conditions we choose the terms indicated under “outdated”.
