## Relevant: wir geben Antworten

### Write punchy messages (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-001`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Aussagekräftig schreiben", p. 2
- **Instruction:** Avoid words that say little, are self-evident or are worn out. Replace general adjectives with concrete information, but only with information from the input. If the input has none, remove the empty adjective or leave the sentence unchanged.
- **Avoid:** `kompetente Berater` (every adviser must be competent), `gut`, `nett`, `zuvorkommend`, `effizient`, `freundlich`
- **Not affected:** fixed greetings, closings and wishes, for example `Freundliche Grüsse`, `Guten Tag`, `Gute Besserung`.

### Stay simple (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-002`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Einfach bleiben", p. 2
- **Instruction:** Concentrate on what is necessary. Avoid repetition and words that repeat what another word already says.
- **Replace:** `nähere Einzelheiten` → `Einzelheiten`, `schlussendlich` → `schliesslich`, `in seltenen Ausnahmen` → `in Ausnahmen`

### Take the customer's perspective (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-003`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Perspektive des Kunden einnehmen", p. 3
- **Instruction:** Avoid the internal view and marketing or business jargon. Drop qualifiers the reader does not need.
- **Avoid:** `Mehrwert`, `Zeitfenster`, `proaktiv`, `zielführend`, `zeitnah`
- **Replace:** `Versicherungsberatung` → `Beratung`, `Versicherungsdeckung` → `Deckung`, `Versicherungsleistung` → `Leistung`, `Kundenbetreuungsteam` → `Betreuungsteam`

## Verständlich: wir kommen auf den Punkt

### Keep sentences short (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-004`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Möglichst kurz halten", p. 3
- **Instruction:** Avoid long and nested sentences, strings of nouns and insertions after an article. Use at most one subordinate clause per sentence. In longer sentences, put the main verb as early as possible.

### Use verbs, not nominalisations (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-005`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Klar sein", p. 4
- **Instruction:** Leave nominalisations to legal texts. Use the verb instead, because it shows more clearly who does what.

### Be credible: no casual word creations (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-006`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Glaubwürdig sein", p. 4
- **Instruction:** Avoid ad-hoc word creations ending in `-ig`, `-mässig` or `-weise`, because they sound too casual. Established words are fine, for example `wichtig`, `günstig`, `beispielsweise`, `möglicherweise`.
- **Replace:** `erstmalig` → `erste` (`nach erstmaliger Untersuchung` → `nach der ersten Untersuchung`), `gesundheitsmässig` → `gesundheitlich`

### Explain foreign words and technical terms (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-007`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Wenn nötig aufklären", p. 5; "Relevant", p. 2
- **Instruction:** Use foreign words, especially anglicisms, sparingly. A foreign word is fine when the German alternative is outdated or unusual (`E-Mail`, not `elektronische Post`). Explain technical terms the reader may not know, in the text itself.
- **Terms that need an explanation, for example:** `ambulante Behandlung`, `stationäre Behandlung`, `Leistungserbringer`, `Dermatologin`, `Unfalldeckung`, `Tarifvertrag`

### Address the reader directly (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-008`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Direkt ansprechen", p. 5
- **Instruction:** Put the reader at the centre. As a rule, do not refer to Helsana by name; use `wir` instead (see also section 12, Helsana the brand).
- **Not affected:** the signature, product and service names (for example `Helsana+`, `Helsana-App`) and the names of legal entities.

## Transparent: wir schreiben so, wie wir sind

### Be realistic: no superlatives (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-009`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Realistisch bleiben", p. 5–6
- **Instruction:** Avoid meaningless superlatives and exaggerating adjectives. Prefer understatement and let the service speak for itself.
- **Avoid:** `ausserordentlich`, `erstrangig`, `exzellent`, `fantastisch`, `die besten`, `die meisten`
- **Not affected:** fixed greetings and closings, for example `Mit besten Grüssen`.

### Be accessible: no relativising words (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-010`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Zugänglich sein", p. 6
- **Instruction:** Avoid modal verbs and modal particles that weaken a statement and create distance.
- **Report:** the particles `wohl` and `eigentlich` used as filler, and `mögen`, `sollen`, `können` when the sentence stays true without them (`Sie können eigentlich von einem breiten Angebot profitieren` → `Sie profitieren von einem breiten Angebot`).
- **Not affected:** modal verbs that state a real option, permission or obligation (`Sie können bis 30. November kündigen`, `Sie müssen das Formular einreichen`), and words that express real uncertainty about a fact. Removing them would turn an uncertain statement into a promise.

### Write in the active voice (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-011`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Aktiv schreiben", p. 6
- **Instruction:** Write in the active voice. Passive sentences are clumsy and hide who acts. Name the actor only if the input makes it clear; otherwise keep the passive.

### Formal address (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-012`)

- **Severity:** mandatory
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Relevant", p. 2
- **Instruction:** Address the reader as `Sie`. The detailed rules are in section 10, Form of address.

### Write out abbreviations (`cpm-kbs-tone-de-013`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language DE, "Relevant", p. 2
- **Instruction:** Write out an abbreviation in full the first time it appears, followed by the abbreviation in brackets, for example `Krankenversicherungsgesetz (KVG)`. After that, the abbreviation alone is fine. Write out an abbreviation only if its meaning is certain; otherwise report it and leave it unchanged.
- **Not affected:** abbreviations that are common in everyday language, for example `CHF`, `z. B.`, `usw.`
