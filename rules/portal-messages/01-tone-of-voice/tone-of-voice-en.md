## Relevant: we provide answers

### Write punchy messages (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-001`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Write punchy messages", p. 2
- **Instruction:** Avoid words that say little, are self-evident or are out of date. Replace general adjectives with concrete information, but only with information from the input.
- **Avoid:** `expert advisors` (every advisor is an expert by definition), `good`, `nice`, `accommodating`, `efficient`, `friendly`
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer |
  |---|---|
  | `an efficient doctor` | `a doctor with long-standing experience` |
  | `a friendly support team` | `a support team that takes your individual needs into account` |

### Stay simple (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-002`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Stay simple", p. 2
- **Instruction:** Concentrate on the essentials. Avoid repetition and words that repeat what another word already says.
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer | Why |
  |---|---|---|
  | `in-depth details` | `details` | Details are in-depth by definition. |
  | `in rare exceptions` | `in exceptions` | Exceptions are always rare. |

### Take the customer's perspective (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-003`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Adopt the perspective of the customer", p. 2–3
- **Instruction:** Avoid the internal view and marketing or business jargon. Drop qualifiers the reader does not need.
- **Avoid:** `added value`, `time window`, `proactive`, `expedient`, `timely`
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer | Why |
  |---|---|---|
  | `insurance consultation` | `consultation` | *Consultation* is enough, as are *coverage* and *benefits*. |
  | `customer support team` | `support team` | |

## Easy to understand: we get to the point

### Keep sentences short (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-004`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Keep sentences as short as possible", p. 3
- **Instruction:** Avoid long and convoluted sentences and strings of nouns. Use at most one subordinate clause per sentence. In longer sentences, put the key verb as early as possible.
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer |
  |---|---|
  | `You need to bear in mind that you need to register with a GP who belongs to our network of doctors.` | `It is important that you register with a GP from our network of doctors.` |
  | `We support you in choosing a doctor, naturopath or therapist recognised by Helsana.` | `Find your doctor, naturopath or therapist. We are happy to help.` |

### Use verbs, not nominalisations (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-005`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Be clear", p. 3–4
- **Instruction:** Leave nominalisations to legal texts. Use the verb instead, because it shows more clearly who does what.
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer |
  |---|---|
  | `A valid health declaration is required for the conclusion of a supplementary insurance contract.` | `You will need a valid health declaration for any supplementary insurance.` |

### Be credible: keep it simple (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-006`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Be credible", p. 4
- **Instruction:** Do not make wording more complicated or more casual than necessary. Prefer the plain word.
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer |
  |---|---|
  | `After the initial examination.` | `After the first examination.` |
  | `Are you doing okay healthwise?` | `Are you in good health?` |

### Explain technical terms (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-007`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Relevant: we provide answers", p. 2
- **Instruction:** Use simple language. Explain any technical term the reader may not know, in the text itself.

### Address the reader directly (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-008`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Address people directly", p. 4
- **Instruction:** Put the reader at the centre. As a rule, do not refer to Helsana by name; use `we` instead (see also section 12, Helsana the brand).
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer |
  |---|---|
  | `Helsana offers supplementary insurance for a wide range of needs.` | `Choose a supplementary insurance for your needs.` |

## Transparent: we write the way we are

### Be realistic: no superlatives (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-009`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Be realistic", p. 5
- **Instruction:** Avoid meaningless superlatives and exaggeration. Prefer understatement and let the service speak for itself.
- **Avoid:** `extraordinary`, `first-rate`, `excellent`, `fantastic`, `the best`, `the most`
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer |
  |---|---|
  | `Our advice is fantastic because we work with the best partners.` | `The quality of our advice is of key importance to us. That is why we only work with partners who are certified by the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority.` |
  | `We have the most customers and a great deal of experience in the healthcare sector.` | `Health has mattered to us for 125 years. You and our more than 2 million customers benefit from a variety of services.` |

### Be accessible: no relativising words (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-010`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Be accessible", p. 5
- **Instruction:** Avoid modal verbs and adverbs that weaken a statement and create distance. Keep them when they express a real option or obligation (see general-002).
- **Avoid:** `like`, `should`, `can`, `probably`, `arguably`, `actually`
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer |
  |---|---|
  | `As a customer, you can actually benefit from a wide range of products and services.` | `As a customer, you benefit from a wide range of products and services.` |

### Write in the active voice (`cpm-kbs-tone-en-011`)

- **Severity:** guideline
- **Source:** Corporate language EN, "Write in the active voice", p. 6
- **Instruction:** Write in the active voice. Passive sentences are clumsy and make actions impersonal. Name the actor only if the input makes it clear (see general-002).
- **Examples:**

  | Avoid | Prefer |
  |---|---|
  | `Your invoice is being processed.` | `We are processing your invoice.` |
  | `Is supplementary insurance required?` | `Would you like supplementary insurance?` |
  | `In such cases, the transport costs would be borne by us.` | `In such cases we would bear the transport costs.` |
