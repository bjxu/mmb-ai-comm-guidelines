These rules cover opening salutations and the associated form of address in German customer correspondence. They do not cover closing greetings, address blocks, signatures or marketing-specific forms of address. Preserve supplied names and titles; do not infer a person's gender, title, preferred form of address or relationship history. Names in examples are illustrative, not recipient data. Replace bracketed placeholders only with information available in the context.

Where a rule below conflicts with another guideline, the more specific rule wins. In particular, apply the documented exceptions only in the circumstances stated.

#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-de-001`

**Instruction:** As a general rule, address customers formally and politely using `Sie` throughout the customer journey.


#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-de-002`

**Instruction:** Use the traditional formal opening salutation. Avoid overly familiar salutations. When the recipient's surname and form of address are known, use the corresponding form below.

**Correct:**

- `Sehr geehrte Frau Keller`
- `Sehr geehrter Herr Müller`


#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-de-003`

**Instruction:** Use `Sehr geehrte Dame, sehr geehrter Herr` when the customer name is unknown. 

**Correct:**

- `Sehr geehrte Dame, sehr geehrter Herr`


#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-de-004`

**Instruction:** Address customers as private individuals, including federal councillors and religious dignitaries. Academic titles such as `Doktor` and `Professor` are the exception. Diplomas do not count as academic titles and must not be included in the salutation.

**Correct examples when the corresponding title and form of address are known:**

- `Sehr geehrter Herr Dr. Muster`
- `Sehr geehrter Herr Professor Muster`
- `Sehr geehrte Frau Professorin Muster`

**Correct examples when the doctoral title and form of address are known but the surname is unknown:**

- `Sehr geehrter Herr Doktor`
- `Sehr geehrte Frau Doktorin`


#### ruleId: `cpm-kbs-salutations-de-006`

**Applies to:** Individual correspondence with a non-binary person.

**Instruction:** A person may, for example, be addressed with `Guten Tag [Vorname] [Nachname]`. Ask the person directly how they wish to be addressed.

**Format:** `Guten Tag [Vorname] [Nachname]`
